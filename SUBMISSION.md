Money Coach

https://github.com/krutarthsathe/money-coach

## Suggested project description

Money Coach is a mobile decision tool for gig and daily-wage workers facing irregular income. A worker enters today's earnings, account balance, hourly take-home pay, and upcoming or spontaneous expenses. The app immediately says whether the expenses are covered. If money is short, it translates the exact gap into two transparent choices: the additional paid hours needed at zero fee, or a minimum cash advance with its fee, total repayment, and effect on the worker's next pay.

The separate forecast calendar uses editable assumptions to show daily, weekly, and monthly cash pressure. Money Coach is built with React Native, Expo, and TypeScript, uses six linked CSV datasets for evidence-backed defaults, and verifies its calculation engines with 16 deterministic scenarios.

## One-line pitch

Turn “I am short” into an understandable choice between hours of work and the real cost of borrowing.

## Demo notes

- Start with **Money is short**.
- Emphasize that the app borrows only the gap and never treats an advance as income.
- Edit one amount to demonstrate that the result is calculated, not hard-coded.
- Show **Everything covered** to prove that extra work and debt drop to zero.
- Finish on the forecast calendar and switch among Daily, Weekly, and Monthly.
